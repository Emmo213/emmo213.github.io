Amherst Rail Park
Preserving History on the Water Level Route

Anchored by the historic 1905 brick Nordson Depot the Amherst Rail Park celebrates more than 170 years of Northern Ohio railroad heritage alongside one of North America’s busiest double track freight and passenger mainlines.

Historic Nordson Depot & the Water Level Route

From 19th century Lake Shore railroading to a thriving civic gathering place and premier Northern Ohio railfan viewing park.

Railroad service through Amherst began in 1853 along the famed shoreline corridor that became the Lake Shore & Michigan Southern Railway and later the New York Central System’s legendary “Water Level Route” connecting New York, Cleveland, Toledo, and Chicago.

In 1905 the railroad constructed Amherst’s brick passenger station featuring classic sandstone trim and broad eaves to shelter travelers along the busy double-track mainline. The depot served passenger trains until 1963, and its architectural and civic significance earned placement on the National Register of Historic Places in 1978.

Preserved and maintained for decades by the Nordson Corporation, the landmark building and surrounding grounds were generously donated to the City of Amherst in 2021. Today the depot is used as the City of Amherst Office on Aging headquarters for hosting community gatherings and senior activities, while the surrounding park provides a playground and is a welcoming and safe destination for railfans, photographers, and visitors watching modern Norfolk Southern and Amtrak operations.

Milestone Timeline

  1. 1853

    Original Mainline Opens

    Railroad tracks open through Amherst on the Cleveland-Toledo shoreline route.

  2. 1905

    Brick Passenger Depot Built

    Lake Shore & Michigan Southern Railway constructs the current brick station.

  3. 1963 & 1978

    Passenger Finale & National Register

    Regular depot passenger stops conclude in 1963; listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1978.

  4. 2021 - Present

    Civic Landmark & Rail Park Vision

    Donated by Nordson Corporation to the City of Amherst in 2021, anchoring Nordson Depot Park and live Virtual Railfan broadcasts.
